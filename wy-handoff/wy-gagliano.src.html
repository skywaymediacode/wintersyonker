What WeHandle

Winters & Yonker handles personal injury claims and nothing else. No real estate closings, no divorces, no wills. That focus covers car accidents, motorcycle and commercial truck collisions, Uber and Lyft crashes, pedestrian and bicycle injuries, slip and fall and other premises cases, construction site injuries, dog bites, traumatic brain injuries, nursing home neglect and abuse, and wrongful death claims brought by surviving families.

What a Florida claim actually covers surprises most people. Beyond emergency care and follow-up treatment, it can include future medical costs, lost wages and lost earning capacity, out-of-pocket expenses, vehicle damage, and non-economic damages for pain, disability, disfigurement and loss of enjoyment of life. A spouse may hold a separate claim for loss of consortium. Those categories are easy to leave money in when nobody itemizes them, which is exactly why a first offer arrives so fast.

You will talk to a lawyer about which of them apply to you, not a case manager working from a script. And if the honest answer is that your case is small, or that you don't need a lawyer at all, we will tell you that on the first call.

When to call

When To Call A Tampa
Personal Injury Lawyer

The honest answer is before you speak to the other driver's insurance company. Florida injury claims run on three separate clocks, and two of them start the moment you are hurt.

14 Days
To protect your PIP benefits

Florida's no-fault law requires you to receive initial medical services and care within 14 days of a crash. Miss that window and your Personal Injury Protection coverage (up to $10,000 with an emergency medical condition diagnosis, $2,500 without one) can be denied outright, leaving you personally responsible for the bills.

2 Years
To file a negligence lawsuit

House Bill 837 cut Florida's negligence deadline from four years to two, effective March 24, 2023. Claims arising on or after that date now expire in two years; wrongful death is two years from the date of death. Claims involving a government entity carry their own, shorter notice requirements.

Day One
Before the evidence is gone

Skid marks wash away in the first Tampa thunderstorm. Vehicles get repaired or sold for salvage. Commercial trucking logs and business surveillance footage are routinely overwritten within weeks. A preservation letter sent early is often the whole difference between proving fault and arguing about it.

The call you should not take

Adjusters ask for a recorded statement early, while you are medicated and before anyone knows whether that shoulder needs surgery. Nothing in Florida law requires you to give one to the other driver's carrier. Once you are a client here, those calls come to us instead of you.

If someone told you it was your fault

Florida also changed how fault is shared. Under the modified comparative negligence standard adopted in 2023, a person found more than 50% at fault recovers nothing. That is precisely why insurers now push blame onto injured people harder than they used to. Being told the wreck was your fault is a reason to call, not a reason not to.

Please note: this page describes Florida law in general terms as of 2026 and is not legal advice. Deadlines, exceptions and how they apply depend on the facts of your case. Confirm yours with an attorney before relying on any date above.

How long do I have to file a claim in Florida?

Florida's deadline for most negligence claims is two years from the date of the injury, and some claims, such as those against a government entity, carry much shorter notice requirements. Call as soon as you can so nothing expires while you're deciding.

Learn More
The insurance company already called me. Should I give a statement?

Call us first. A recorded statement given early, without advice, is one of the most common ways a good claim gets damaged. Once you're a client, those calls come to us instead of you.

Learn More
What if I was partly at fault?

You may still be able to recover. Florida uses a modified comparative negligence rule, and fault is often assigned very differently once the evidence is actually examined. Don't assume you have no case because someone told you it was yours.

Learn More
Do I have to go to court?

Most cases settle without a trial. We prepare every file as though it will be tried, because that is what moves an adjuster's number. You'd only see a courtroom if the insurer refuses to be reasonable, and we'd talk that through with you first.
